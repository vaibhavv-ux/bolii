Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At BOLI (https://bolii.netlify.app), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

  • Public Marketing Details: When placing a bid, you provide a company or founder name and an optional website URL. These are displayed publicly on the live board and historical ledger.
  • Transaction Data: Razorpay order IDs and payment reference identifiers to confirm and log your bid.
  • Support Communications: Email messages sent to our official support email for customer assistance.

2. Payment Information Security

We NEVER collect, view, or store your credit/debit card numbers, CVV, bank account passwords, or UPI PINs. All financial transactions are handled directly through Razorpay Payment Gateway, which uses bank-grade 128-bit SSL encryption and complies with PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To broadcast and display your company's bid on the live BOLI board, spotlight crown, and ticker.
  • To verify transaction validity and prevent spam or abuse.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries and provide technical assistance.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share transaction metadata with our payment processor (Razorpay) as required to facilitate payments.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use standard, anonymous session cookies solely to maintain website performance, prevent DDoS attacks, and provide low-latency leaderboard updates.

6. Contact & Data Inquiries

If you have any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please email: digitalplus077@gmail.com.
